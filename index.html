Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?
Message us on WhatsApp or use the order button on this page with your preferred package, quantity and delivery address. We confirm your order and cooking time right away.
How fresh is the food really?
Everything is cooked the same day it's delivered — nothing is pre-made and frozen. Most orders reach you within two hours of leaving the kitchen.
Can I customize my order or spice level?
Yes. Let us know your preferred spice level, or swap out an item you'd rather skip, when you place your order.
Do you cater for events or bulk orders?
Absolutely. Send us your event date, headcount and package preference and we'll put together a bulk quote for you.
What areas do you deliver to?
We currently deliver across Nairobi and Kiambu County, with same-day delivery windows. Message us to confirm delivery to your area.
